# Google's OKF - The New Way to Structure Your Knowledge for Agents

## Summary

The source discusses Google's Open Knowledge Format (OKF), a new standard for structuring knowledge for AI agents using Markdown. It highlights how OKF changes SEO by making business knowledge accessible to agents, allows selling proprietary knowledge through OKF bundles, and provides guidance on using tools like NotebookLM and Gemini to learn and implement OKF. The format uses YAML front matter for metadata and organizes knowledge into single Markdown files representing concepts, with index and log files for structure. The speaker discusses the concept of OKF (Organized Knowledge Framework) as a way to organize and maintain knowledge, contrasting it with traditional methods like RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). They mention practical examples such as using OKF with BigQuery data and triggered playbooks. The speaker also references Andrej Karpathy's LLM wiki, where the language model maintains and updates a persistent knowledge base. They highlight the potential for OKF to become a new schema for organizing knowledge, leading to future revenue streams and the creation of a network where expertise is valued. The speaker also mentions the idea of 'semantic unbaking' as a term provided by Gemini.

## Main ideas

- OKF is a standardized format for structuring knowledge for AI agents using Markdown.

- Proprietary knowledge can be sold as OKF bundles, allowing integration into other systems.
- OKF uses YAML front matter for metadata and organizes knowledge into single Markdown files representing concepts.
- Index and log files help structure OKF bundles, with tools like Git, Notion, and Obsidian recommended for management.
- OKF is a framework for organizing knowledge, allowing for structured and persistent storage of information.
- OKF differs from RAG by having the language model maintain and update a knowledge base incrementally.
- The speaker references Andrej Karpathy's LLM wiki, where the model builds and maintains a persistent knowledge structure.
- OKF has potential as a new schema for organizing knowledge, leading to business opportunities and revenue streams.
- The speaker discusses the idea of 'semantic unbaking,' where knowledge is shared and used in a more natural, human-like way.
- Agents can discover OKF bundles through LLMs.txt, which directs them to the knowledge sources.
