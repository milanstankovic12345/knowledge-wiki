# Why Personal Knowledge Management Compounds

## Summary

The article discusses personal knowledge management, emphasizing the importance of linking notes for easier rediscovery and the use of spaced repetition for better memory retention. It also mentions the compounding value of a note-taking system and raises an open question about delegating wiki maintenance to a language model.

## Main ideas

- Personal knowledge management involves capturing, organizing, and revisiting learned information.
- Linking notes enhances their discoverability compared to isolated folders.
- Spaced repetition improves memory retention by reviewing material at increasing intervals.
- A continuously maintained wiki avoids the failure of abandoned note piles.
- The article questions how much wiki maintenance can be delegated to a language model without losing trust in the results.

## Important claims

- Reviewing a fact after one day, one week, and one month makes it more likely to be remembered than reading it once.
- A note-taking system compounds in value as new notes link to existing ones, making both more useful.
