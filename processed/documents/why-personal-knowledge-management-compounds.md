# Why Personal Knowledge Management Compounds

- Author: Test Author
- Captured: 2026-10-19

## Content

This is a short, invented article used to test the ingestion pipeline.

Personal knowledge management is the practice of capturing, organising and revisiting what you learn so that it is available when you need it. Notes that are linked to each other are easier to rediscover than notes filed in isolated folders.

Spaced repetition is a technique where material is reviewed at increasing intervals. The author claims that reviewing a fact after one day, one week and one month makes it far more likely to be remembered than reading it once.

The author argues that a note-taking system compounds in value: every new note that links to an existing one makes both more useful. A wiki that is maintained continuously avoids the usual failure of abandoned note piles.
